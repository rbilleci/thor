# Maintain Assurance Protocol

Edit only requested protocol contracts. Keep the dispatcher limited to assignment, optional updates, bounded finding-materiality and Architect- or resolver-choice adjudication, decision-response relay, terminal handling, and the no-workflow-file rule. Require compact task-specific assignments that retain the complete baseline but do not copy or paraphrase the general slicer protocol. Require the dispatcher during normal operation to wait for and message only the retained slicer and to receive status through its `UPDATE`s; prohibit routine listing, inspection, or ingestion of descendant reviewer threads or results. Permit descendant inspection only for platform-confirmed owner-loss diagnosis or stop confirmation. Prohibit the dispatcher from changing the requirements baseline or an applicable contract, accepting risk, declaring `COMPLETE`, extending the repair-round limit, directing implementation, invoking the Architect, or performing review. Do not move other maintenance governance into dispatcher or runtime agent prompts.

Require the dispatcher to define the requirements baseline as the independently verifiable outcome, scope, non-goals, constraints, and acceptance criteria. Require each compact, task-specific `ASSIGNMENT` to include that complete baseline, decision authority, dependencies, base commit, assigned branch, checkout, and a new slice identifier, but prohibit it from repeating the slicer’s general protocol. Require the assignment to grant the dispatcher authority to determine an escalated finding's materiality and choose among Architect- or resolver-supplied interpretations only when the decision preserves the baseline and every applicable contract; reserve changes to either for external authority. Before sending it, require the dispatcher to verify that the current branch equals the assigned branch, `HEAD` equals the base commit, and `git status --porcelain` is empty; on failure, require `BLOCKED` without altering the checkout. Require the dispatcher to launch one `slicer` subagent as the Slice Owner and make it the checkout’s only writer. Prohibit another Git worktree or branch.

Apply the frozen-candidate definition above. Require the Slice Owner to commit the candidate on the current branch before assurance and `COMPLETE`, pause writes throughout systemic review and the complete focused wave, and verify the recorded commit and clean checkout before accepting a result. Before `COMPLETE`, require the Slice Owner to verify the reviewed candidate and a clean checkout. Require the next slice to use the completed commit as its base. Do not model candidate handoff through trees, merges, snapshot machinery, or another checkout.

Require one architect-first phase after admission and before the slice's first checkout mutation. Require the slicer to pause writes and invoke a fresh read-only Architect with the complete baseline, decision authority, dependencies, base, repository instructions, authoritative documents, relevant implementation, operational context, and discovery evidence. Require matching outcome/base, exactly one supported status, and each Architecture Result section exactly once; define a complete plan as affected surfaces, generated-output consequences, ordered work, ownership, constraints, and validation mappings. Require ready statuses to have complete plans and validation obligations with no decision or evidence gap; reject a mismatched, malformed, incomplete, or otherwise unusable result before writing. Require the trusted-but-fallible agent assumption: assess correctness rather than hypothetical dishonesty unless supplied evidence places an actor outside the trust boundary; do not add hostile-agent authentication or adversarial-agent protocols.

Require the slicer to validate the result in its platform channel, then exclusively create a uniquely named temporary artifact outside the checkout, write the exact UTF-8 result once, calculate and reread-verify its SHA-256 digest, confirm write capability closed, and retain only reference/digest in slicer-local state. Never edit or replace an artifact in place. Treat create, write, reread, verification, or close failure as partial artifact failure: delete and retry only at a new unique location; block on unavailable storage; delete a non-ready artifact after its route. Retain active ready artifacts through their readers, delete superseded artifacts after readers return or stop, and before terminal deletion confirm every reader returned or stopped; otherwise retain artifact and remain paused or `BLOCKED`. Treat already absent as cleaned; a resumable cleanup outage pauses termination and a non-resumable one returns `BLOCKED` with a reference-free platform-cleanup condition. Missing or mismatched accepted evidence pauses writes, invalidates dependent assurance evidence, and requires fresh Architect/artifact before further mutation or assurance. Continue with retained local state after resumable platform unavailability; follow stop handling and return `BLOCKED` on platform-reported non-resumable failure.

Permit artifact reference/digest only to descendant systemic or planned focused reviewers that need design evidence; each verifies digest and identity and reports an evidence gap on inaccessible, missing, mismatched, malformed, or wrong-identity evidence. Never route the Architecture Result, an excerpt presented as one, its temporary reference, or digest through Dispatcher messages. For Architect `DECISION_REQUIRED`, permit only a distinct bounded question with viable interpretations, governing constraints, and consequences; the Dispatcher determines whether its decision lies within assigned authority and preserves baseline/contracts, then relays the exact binding determination. The slicer checks only matching context and supplied option and never re-adjudicates a valid determination. Resolver `RESOLVED`, Dispatcher, and external decisions require fresh Architect; route resolver `DECISION_REQUIRED` through the bounded dispatcher path; obtain exact `INDETERMINATE` evidence or block. `INDETERMINATE` Architect results require named no-edit evidence then one fresh Architect; repeated or unavailable evidence/result blocks. Ordinary implementation, generation, validation, and repair writes within accepted design do not re-invoke Architect. Re-architect only after material invalidation of design responsibilities, contracts, ownership, trust, lifecycle, concurrency, deployment, recovery, or validation obligations, accepted-evidence mismatch, authoritative decision, or relevant baseline/base change. Require implementation and validation against accepted result and no Architect implementation certification. Preserve resumable-slice and non-resumable-interruption rules, authoritative-source validation, resolver scope, and no generic governance manifest.

Permit one `ASSIGNMENT`, zero or more `UPDATE`s, and one `TERMINAL` per slice. Route `ASSIGNMENT` to the Slice Owner and route its messages back for the same slice. Require every `UPDATE` to include the stable fields `Slice`, `Phase`, `Candidate`, and `Attention` exactly once; require `Phase` to be `freeze`, `review-start`, `repair-start`, `blocker`, `decision`, or `status`; require `Candidate` to be a frozen candidate commit or `None`; and require `Attention` to state one dispatcher-relevant condition or `None`. Limit `UPDATE` to freeze or review start, repair start, blocker or decision, or requested status. Prohibit additions to a non-decision `UPDATE`. Require every Dispatcher-bound `UPDATE` and `TERMINAL`, including attention, assurance, stop, and paused-state fields, to exclude Architecture Result content, temporary reference, and digest; state artifact failure only as a reference-free condition, and reject a message that leaks an identifier. Permit a decision `UPDATE` to add only a reference-free raw finding or resolver result and governing evidence, or the distinct bounded Architect decision request. Require the Dispatcher to determine whether a choice preserves baseline/contracts within its assigned authority, then relay the exact matching determination. The slicer mechanically checks identity and supplied option and treats a valid in-authority determination as binding; it must not re-adjudicate it. Permit the Dispatcher only to determine whether complete proof satisfies the finding obligation or select one Architect- or resolver-supplied interpretation. For a decision outside authority, require the external authority's exact response. Prohibit every other inferred authority decision. Require `TERMINAL` to certify that neither the owner nor started work can write to the checkout. Verify each message against the active assignment and require a candidate commit for `COMPLETE`. Retain the assignment until accepting `TERMINAL` or completing confirmed owner-loss handling. Clear it before another `ASSIGNMENT`. Do not persist workflow state or reports.

Retain one end-to-end Slice Owner per slice, a frozen commit, a mandatory independent systemic gate, a resolver, focused auditors, fast validation during editing, and slower validation after stabilization. Require systemic assurance to review raw candidate and independently established evidence first, without any previous reviewer conclusion, and to inspect every relevant authoritative document and implementation surface needed for a complete system-wide determination. Require systemic assurance to evaluate end-to-end relationships and emergent behavior, and focused reviewers to evaluate lens-owned invariants. Treat materiality as a finding obligation, not a severity label. Require every reported finding to name the violated acceptance criterion, outcome invariant, or applicable contract; connect the exact candidate to a feasible current-context scenario; identify the observable failure; and explain why accepting the candidate would leave the named requirement unsatisfied. Require an exact evidence gap for a missing or conflicting fact and omission when evidence does not establish every element, including minor, speculative, theoretical, preference-based, and hardening-only concerns. Permit systemic and focused review to inspect the same evidence, but prohibit the systemic determination from substituting for focused review of a materially changed lens-owned invariant. Require the systemic reviewer to produce a focused review plan only when `Findings` and `Evidence Gaps` are both `None`, and require the smallest sufficient lens set. Select a lens only when the candidate materially changes a directly owned behavior, contract, or invariant, supplied evidence makes a concrete failure mode reachable, the consequence would materially affect the outcome or an applicable contract, and the lens supplies an independent specialized determination of that distinct risk. When risks overlap, select the lens that most directly owns the root failure and omit lenses that would review only another consequence of the same causal sequence; another planned lens justifies omission only when the omitted lens owns no separate changed material invariant. Prohibit selection based only on an imaginable issue, related files, technologies, keywords, hypothetical future scale, missing evidence, or cross-boundary effects. Permit `Selection: None` only when the candidate materially changes no focused lens-owned invariant. Require missing evidence to produce an evidence gap instead of broader selection. Require every finding to enter `REPAIR` or `DECISION` resolution, and restart systemic assurance for every replacement candidate.

After a matching systemic pass, require the Slice Owner to accept the plan only when it names distinct canonical reviewers, provides the four-part trigger and distinct contribution for every selection, rejects overlapping consequence-only selections, and provides candidate-specific omission evidence that each unselected lens owns no materially changed invariant or owns only a secondary consequence of a root failure directly owned by a planned lens. Reject systemic coverage as omission evidence. Prohibit the Slice Owner from adding, removing, or substituting reviewers. When the plan selects reviewers, require one focused wave containing every planned auditor for the frozen candidate. Require the owner to start members until no agent execution slot remains, keep unstarted members pending in the same wave, and start one whenever the platform reports an available slot before later assurance work. Lack of slots must not split the wave, change its candidate, permit omission, or constitute failure. Define eventual start and join as conditional on the platform eventually reporting capacity and reviewer completion or inability; prohibit a claim of platform liveness while it supplies none. During an external stall, retain pending members, keep the candidate immutable, and prohibit repair or `COMPLETE`. Give each member only needed raw evidence, withhold the systemic-review conclusion, require an exact candidate match and reviewed revision, and retain early results. Define wave completion as every member returning a matching result. Require the Slice Owner, not the systemic reviewer, to reconcile overlapping focused findings after the complete wave. Do not cancel or delay a pending member because another reports a finding. Require each auditor to return `Findings` and `Evidence Gaps`; resolve each gap, then require a fresh matching result. Restart systemic assurance if new evidence changes plan applicability. If the platform reports that a planned auditor cannot start or return, require completed stop handling and `BLOCKED` unless that auditor supplied a matching result. Skip the wave only when the plan proves that the candidate materially changes no focused lens-owned invariant.

After a systemic finding result or complete focused wave with findings, require the Slice Owner to verify every finding’s materiality proof, reject a result that omits an element, and require a fresh matching result from its reviewer. Treat that check as mechanical rather than a materiality determination. When complete proof leaves a genuine materiality dispute, require a decision `UPDATE` to the dispatcher with the raw result and governing evidence. Require a corrected same-candidate result when the dispatcher determines that the concern is not a finding, and do not count that correction as a repair round. Require `BLOCKED` when an independent role cannot return a usable result.

Set the repair-round counter to zero after the initial candidate commit. Define one ordinary repair round as one validated and committed replacement candidate produced in response to one systemic finding result or one complete focused wave with findings. Require the Slice Owner to deduplicate equivalent findings and group `REPAIR` findings by root cause and dependency. Apply all mutually compatible authorized groups in one repair batch; separate groups only for conflict, dependency, or isolated validation needed for a defensible result. Serialize all repair writes. Route `DECISION` findings through the resolver and route its `DECISION_REQUIRED` result to the Dispatcher. On a matching binding Dispatcher determination or exact external decision relayed through it, reset an initialized counter to zero immediately before re-architecture, maximum check, or replacement work; do not grant reset discretion to the Dispatcher. A required replacement validates, commits, and increments the reset counter to one; a corrected same-candidate result leaves it zero and consumes no round. A pre-candidate counter remains uninitialized. Before an ordinary repair when the counter equals three, require `FAILED` with remaining admissible findings and counter evidence. Prohibit the Slice Owner and Dispatcher from extending the maximum. Keep the Slice Owner as the checkout’s only writer.

Require `COMPLETE` to have matching systemic and planned focused results with `Findings: None` and `Evidence Gaps: None` for the reviewed candidate. When `Selection` is `None`, require only the systemic pass.

If a called role cannot return, require the Slice Owner to request a platform stop and return `BLOCKED`. Require the dispatcher to keep the active Slice Owner running until `TERMINAL`, continue the same Slice Owner when the platform can resume it, and not treat elapsed time, absence of an `UPDATE`, or a resumable platform pause as owner loss. During normal operation, require the dispatcher to wait for and message only the retained Slice Owner and prohibit it from routinely listing, inspecting, or ingesting descendant reviewer threads or results. When the platform reports that the retained Slice Owner cannot continue the active slice, permit descendant inspection only to obtain platform-confirmed owner-loss diagnosis or stop confirmation, then require the dispatcher to request a platform stop for that owner and slice. Start no other writer until platform confirmation states that the retained Slice Owner and all work it started cannot write to checkout and that their slice-local Architecture Result artifacts are cleaned. Treat any other confirmation as missing, require `BLOCKED`, and preserve checkout. If the Slice Owner returns `TERMINAL` before confirmation, require the dispatcher to handle that result and ignore later stop confirmation. Otherwise, after confirmation, require the dispatcher to rerun original admission checks. If they pass, require the dispatcher to clear assignment and assign outstanding outcome as a new slice. If they fail, require `BLOCKED` with failed check and preserve checkout. Do not create retry scheduling or recovery state.
