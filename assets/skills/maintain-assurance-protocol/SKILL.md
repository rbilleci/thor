# Maintain Assurance Protocol

## Scope and authority

Edit only requested protocol contracts. Keep the Work Dispatcher limited to assignment, compact updates, terminal handling, and determining an escalated finding’s materiality or selecting only among Architect-supplied interpretations when that preserves the requirements baseline and every applicable contract. External authority decides every other baseline or contract change. The Dispatcher does not invoke the Architect, design, implement, review, select reviewers, accept risk, extend rounds, or direct another writer. It waits for and messages only the retained Slice Owner, except for platform-confirmed owner-loss diagnosis or stop confirmation.

Use `assets/definitions/assignment.md` as the canonical definition of the requirements baseline and `ASSIGNMENT` fields. Require each `ASSIGNMENT` to conform to that definition without restating the Slice Owner protocol. Before assignment, require assigned branch, base, and clean status to match. One Slice Owner writes one shared checkout; do not create a worktree, branch, workflow artifact, generic governance manifest, validator script, retry state, or report.

## Architecture and evidence

Require one fresh read-only Architect after admission and before the first checkout mutation. It receives complete baseline, authority, dependencies, base, instructions, documents, implementation, operations, and discovery. Require it to inspect applicable repository instructions, derive bounded discovery questions from the outcome, acceptance criteria, non-goals, constraints, affected behavior, interfaces, data, and operations, enumerate only in-scope candidate sources, conduct targeted read-only discovery, and follow only baseline- or source-relevant authoritative references. Prohibit an unbounded repository audit, external research, secret or personal-data access, and generic-label discovery. Require matching outcome and base, exactly one supported status, singleton required sections, complete plan, generated-output consequences, ownership, constraints, validation obligations, and no unresolved gap for a ready result. A ready result constrains implementation but never certifies it. Ordinary implementation, generation, validation, and repair within accepted design do not invoke the Architect. Re-architect only after material invalidation of responsibility, contract, ownership, trust, lifecycle, concurrency, deployment, recovery, validation, accepted evidence, baseline, or base.

Require every ready Architecture Result to contain one top-level `## Evidence Basis` section with exactly one `### Discovery Scope`, one `### Consulted Sources`, and one `### Missing or Conflicting Evidence`. The scope identifies baseline-derived discovery questions, in-scope candidate routes, followed authoritative references, and a stopping boundary. Each consulted source appears once with `Reference`, `Authority class`, `Relevance`, and `Supports`; `Supports` identifies a determination, design decision, invariant, validation obligation, or assurance-lens selection or omission. Allow only `governing baseline/contract`, `operational requirement`, `implementation evidence`, and `non-authoritative context`. Implementation evidence and context describe current state or orientation; neither creates or overrides a governing requirement without authority evidence. Ready status requires complete traceability and `Missing or Conflicting Evidence: None`. An exact missing or conflicting required fact, unreadable applicable source, or unsupported material conclusion requires `INDETERMINATE` with the attempted in-scope route. The Slice Owner rejects a missing, duplicate, malformed, untraceable, unsupported, or unresolved Evidence Basis before writing, freezing, or assurance.

Require the Architect to determine the complete applicable canonical assurance-lens partition in every ready result. The result contains one top-level `## Applicable Assurance Lenses` section with one `### Selection` and one `### Omissions`; together they form the complete duplicate-free canonical partition. Every selected canonical reviewer, including `lens-systemic-assurance-reviewer`, states its directly owned materially changed behavior or invariant, reachable candidate-specific failure, material consequence, and distinct specialized contribution. Every omitted canonical reviewer states candidate-specific evidence that it owns no materially changed invariant or only a secondary consequence of a selected root failure. `Selection: None` requires all canonical lenses in omissions and no changed canonical invariant. The Slice Owner verifies the partition and may not add, remove, or substitute reviewers.

Treat a change to required Architecture Result structure, required sections, Evidence Basis semantics, or accepted lens-partition semantics as material invalidation of every earlier Architecture Result evidence. Pause implementation, repair, and assurance; do not rewrite or reinterpret an immutable artifact; and require fresh Architect evidence and a new write-once artifact before work resumes.

Validate accepted Architect results through the trusted platform channel. Create a unique temporary artifact outside checkout, write exact UTF-8 once, calculate and reread-verify SHA-256, close writes, and retain only reference and digest locally. Never replace in place. A create, write, reread, verification, or close failure cleans partial output, pauses writes, and permits exactly one new-location replacement; replacement failure cleans output and blocks. Retain ready artifacts through readers. Readers receive only reference and digest, verify digest and identity, and report inaccessible, missing, mismatched, malformed, or wrong-identity evidence as a gap. Missing active evidence invalidates dependent assurance and requires fresh Architect evidence. Delete artifacts only after readers return or stop; preserve existing resumable and non-resumable cleanup and platform-failure routes. Never include result content, reference, or digest in Dispatcher-bound messages.

`DECISION_REQUIRED` and `INDETERMINATE` do not authorize mutation. An Architect decision request contains only bounded question, interpretations, constraints, and consequences. Before round-three assurance-wave start, Dispatcher applies a valid matching determination once per unresolved context; exact duplicates are no-ops and nonidentical determinations are rejected. After round-three wave start, reject every decision delivery. `INDETERMINATE` needs named no-edit evidence and one fresh Architect; an unavailable or repeated result blocks. An authoritative decision requires fresh Architect evidence.

## Candidate, reviews, and rounds

Treat workflow agents and platform results as trusted but fallible. Verify identity, completeness, evidence consistency, and behavior; assess correctness rather than hypothetical malice unless evidence changes the trust boundary. A frozen candidate contains the baseline, base, and complete commit. The Slice Owner commits it before assurance and completion; identity changes invalidate evidence. Pause writes during every assurance wave and verify branch, candidate, and clean checkout before accepting results.

A finding proves that the exact candidate violates a named acceptance criterion, invariant, or applicable contract in a feasible scenario. It names the requirement, candidate connection, observable failure, and acceptance consequence. Classify it as `REPAIR` or `DECISION`. Record exact missing or conflicting facts as `Evidence Gaps`, not unproved findings. Preference, speculation, hardening, or severity alone is not a finding.

One assurance wave contains exactly the canonical reviewers named in the Architect’s Selection for one frozen candidate. Start selected members from raw candidate and independently established evidence until capacity exhausts; retain pending members in the same wave and start each when capacity returns. No reviewer receives another conclusion. Capacity exhaustion may leave members pending, but no early finding, gap, or completion may cancel, delay, split, or sequence another member. Keep the candidate immutable through stalls. Wait for a matching candidate result from every selected member; reject a mismatch, require fresh matching evidence, and prohibit it from driving repair or completion. Resolve a gap with a fresh matching result from the affected reviewer. A selected member unavailable independently of capacity that cannot start or return requires stop handling and `BLOCKED` unless it returned a match. Reconcile overlaps only after the complete non-empty wave. A non-empty wave passes only when every matching result has no findings and no gaps. A valid `Selection: None` produces an empty wave that starts no reviewer, awaits no result, and passes only from accepted Architect evidence that no canonical lens owns a materially changed invariant.

One review-and-repair round is one fresh assurance wave for one candidate. A completed-wave finding is the stopping result; gap resolution, same-candidate correction, extra validation, and duplicate determinations remain in that round. Start round one with the initial wave and advance only when starting a fresh wave for a validated committed repair replacement. In rounds one and two, mechanically verify each finding, deduplicate by root cause and dependency, repair compatible authorized findings in one serialized batch, validate, commit one replacement, and re-review. Route an external-authority `DECISION` through `DECISION_REQUIRED` and unchanged Dispatcher relay, then obtain fresh Architect evidence before repair or a replacement wave. Before round three, route only genuine materiality disputes and Architect-supplied interpretations through the bounded Dispatcher window. In round three, every complete baseline-preserving finding is a repair obligation without materiality escalation; repair compatible obligations in one serialized validated batch, create a terminal direct-child commit, map every finding to change and validation, and complete without post-repair review. Return `FAILED` for conflict, demonstrated infeasibility, or required outside authority, and `BLOCKED` for missing evidence, unusable roles, platform failure, or unavailable validation. Never start a fourth round.

`matching-pass` requires clean matching results from every member of the terminal candidate’s Architect-constrained wave. `final-repair` identifies the reviewed round-three commit and a clean validated direct-child terminal commit with complete finding-to-change-to-validation mapping, and states that no matching independent pass exists. Preserve materiality, raw-evidence independence, candidate identity, security, artifact, stop, cleanup, and single-writer guarantees.

## Protocol messages and maintenance

Permit one assignment, zero or more updates, and one terminal per slice. Every update has `Slice`, `Phase`, `Candidate`, and `Attention` exactly once; phase is `freeze`, `review-start`, `repair-start`, `blocker`, `decision`, or `status`. Send updates only for those events. A decision update adds only a reference-free raw finding and governing evidence or the bounded Architect request. Terminal handling validates the assignment envelope and matching-pass or final-repair basis without conducting assurance. The terminal certifies that neither the owner nor started work can write. Preserve active assignment until terminal acceptance or confirmed owner-loss handling.

Edit only `assets/`; generate projections through Thor. Keep generated agent definitions at or below the repository limit measured by `wc -w`; report actual counts. Before freeze, run `git diff --check`, `cargo run --locked -p thor-build -- validate`, `cargo run --locked -p thor-build -- transform`, and `cargo run --locked -p thor-build -- transform --check`. Parse every Codex agent TOML with Python 3 `tomllib`, verify changed paths, and search source and generated contracts for obsolete role names, candidate-freeze contradictions, prohibited scheduling or checkout-lifecycle terms, and forbidden size-limit references outside this maintenance skill.

For an assurance-protocol change, verify source and generated contracts retain trusted-but-fallible handling, fresh Architect-before-mutation, all four Architect statuses, accepted-result implementation, material-invalidation re-architecture, no implementation certification, write-once artifact lifecycle, reference/digest reader handoff, cleanup, dispatcher leakage prohibition, exactly-once decisions, round identity, direct-child final repair, and no fourth round. Verify ready Architect results and Slice Owner acceptance require one top-level `## Evidence Basis` section with one `### Discovery Scope`, one `### Consulted Sources`, and one `### Missing or Conflicting Evidence`; a bounded baseline-derived scope; one complete fielded source entry per consulted source; only the four authority classes; material traceability; ready-status rejection when required discovery evidence is missing or conflicting; and material invalidation with fresh Architect evidence before implementation, repair, or assurance when required-result structure, sections, Evidence Basis semantics, or partition semantics change. Verify ready Architect results and Slice Owner acceptance also require one top-level `## Applicable Assurance Lenses` section with one `### Selection` and one `### Omissions` and an exhaustive duplicate-free partition of every canonical lens. Verify the Architect owns exhaustive applicable-lens selection and omissions; every canonical reviewer declares the shared reviewer template and returns only revision, findings, and evidence gaps; all selected wave members start capacity-aware from raw evidence without conclusions or gating; every selected matching member must return before repair or completion; and `Selection: None` creates an empty wave only when accepted Architect evidence establishes that no canonical lens owns a materially changed invariant. Verify no source or generated contract retains the removed decision role. Run formatter, strict Clippy, workspace tests, build, build validation, transform checks, and the complete matching assurance wave selected by the accepted Architect result. Do not create a validator script. Repair material defects without adding lifecycle or identity machinery.
