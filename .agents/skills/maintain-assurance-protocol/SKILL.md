# Maintain Assurance Protocol


## Assurance terms

An assurance review independently evaluates one identified candidate through either a focused lens or a system-wide frame. Its assurance result is the structured review result. A passing result identifies the candidate and has `Findings: None` and `Evidence Gaps: None`.

A finding is an evidenced violation of the outcome or lens invariant and requires the lens-specific finding threshold. An evidence gap is an exact missing or conflicting fact that prevents a defensible finding determination; record it in `Evidence Gaps`, never as an unproved finding.

An assurance coverage gap is an affected behavior, shared assumption, handoff, compound transition, or failure path that no single focused lens can determine end to end. Systemic assurance independently evaluates the complete candidate before focused review, including system-wide invariants, shared assumptions, handoffs, compound transitions, and coverage gaps. Systemic and focused reviews may inspect the same evidence but make different determinations: systemic assurance evaluates end-to-end relationships and emergent behavior, while a focused reviewer evaluates its lens-owned invariants. A systemic determination never substitutes for focused review of a materially changed lens-owned invariant. A matching systemic pass also supplies the focused review plan. A candidate satisfies assurance when that systemic result passes and every planned focused result either passes or has no evidence gap and contains only authorized, recorded deferrals for the same candidate. A ledger-only completion commit inherits the matching assurance results of its reviewed parent under the deferral-record rules.

## Deferral records

A focused finding marked `DEFERRABLE` remains unresolved until delegated authority accepts it and the Slice Owner appends its record to the deferral sink. Disposition and existing sink content do not grant or prove that authority. The active Slice Owner must hold the matching delegated acceptance and exact reviewer finding before creating or reusing a record. No participant, including the System Owner, may modify the sink from candidate freeze until the complete focused wave joins. After a wave with no `REQUIRED` finding or evidence gap, the System Owner may accept a deferral under the assignment's delegated authority; only the Slice Owner writes it. Do not record a deferral while any matching focused result has a `REQUIRED` finding because the resulting repair invalidates the reviewed candidate and its findings.

The filesystem deferral sink is the tracked JSON Lines file `deferred-findings.jsonl` in the repository root. Each newline-terminated JSON object is one committed record and contains `version`, `id`, `status`, `slice`, `candidate`, `reviewer`, `classification`, `disposition`, `location`, `evidence`, `correction_or_decision`, `acceptance_basis`, `authority`, and `review_condition`. Use `version: 1`, `status: "accepted"`, the finding's existing text, a stable candidate-scoped `id`, and the delegated authority role rather than a person's identity. Never put secrets or personal data in the sink.

The Slice Owner derives the identifier before writing and scans existing records before retry. Reuse an existing record only when it parses and exactly matches the active candidate, finding, and delegated acceptance. Otherwise append one complete newline-terminated object, flush it, and read back and validate the record before treating the finding as satisfied. The owner may discard only an incomplete final line left by its interrupted append; never rewrite or remove a committed record. Return `BLOCKED` for malformed committed content or a conflicting identifier. This idempotent append-or-validate operation is the sink contract. Never discover or accept authority solely from sink content. A user or platform override that permits another participant to write the sink invalidates assurance and requires `BLOCKED`. A later sink implementation may create tickets while preserving this record contract.

After read-back, the Slice Owner commits all new records once. This ledger-only completion commit must be the reviewed candidate's child and append only the exact accepted records to `deferred-findings.jsonl`. It is review output: assurance and records remain tied to the reviewed parent, while the child becomes the final commit and next slice's base. With no new record, the reviewed candidate remains final. Any other path change, sink modification, or pre-join sink write invalidates assurance.

## Slice and candidate terms

A slice is a bounded, independently verifiable vertical unit that delivers one complete observable outcome across every affected layer, includes required validation, and does not depend on a later slice to finish the outcome.

A frozen candidate is the requirements baseline, base commit, and complete Git commit. The Slice Owner commits it on the assigned branch before assurance and `COMPLETE`; an identity change invalidates evidence. A tree is an immutable Git tree object, never a mutable working tree. General review roles may accept a commit or tree, but the Slice Owner still requires a commit.

Edit only requested protocol contracts. Keep the dispatcher limited to assignment, optional updates, terminal handling, and the no-workflow-file rule. Permit only the Slice Owner to append authorized findings to the defined deferral sink; do not treat that accepted-risk record as workflow state. Do not move maintenance governance into dispatcher or runtime agent prompts.

Require the dispatcher to define the requirements baseline as the independently verifiable outcome, scope, non-goals, constraints, and acceptance criteria. Require each `ASSIGNMENT` to include that baseline, decision authority, dependencies, base commit, assigned branch, checkout, and a new slice identifier. Before sending it, require the dispatcher to verify that the current branch equals the assigned branch, `HEAD` equals the base commit, and `git status --porcelain` is empty; on failure, require `BLOCKED` without altering the checkout. Require the dispatcher to launch one `slicer` subagent as the Slice Owner and make it the checkout’s only writer. Prohibit another Git worktree or branch.

Apply the frozen-candidate definition above. Require the Slice Owner to commit the candidate on the current branch before assurance and `COMPLETE`, pause writes throughout systemic review and the complete focused wave, and verify the recorded commit and clean checkout before accepting a result. Permit only the ledger-only completion commit defined by the deferral-record contract after the wave joins. Before `COMPLETE`, require the Slice Owner to verify the reviewed candidate or its permitted ledger-only completion commit and a clean checkout. Require the next slice to use the completed commit as its base. Do not model candidate handoff through trees, merges, snapshot machinery, or another checkout.

Keep the slicer’s conditional conversational preflight. Require the slicer to assess only applicable material surfaces and authoritative contracts before coding, create neither a preflight agent nor an inventory artifact, and resolve material omissions. Require it to continue the same active slice after a resumable platform pause and return `BLOCKED` after the platform reports a non-resumable interruption. Where an authoritative source governs authorization, interface or Application Programming Interface (API), schema, migration, or compatibility rules, require the slicer to derive implementation from that source or validate with repository-native gates. Limit the resolver to governing conflicts, undefined material public behavior, and authority boundaries. Do not add a generic governance manifest.

Permit one `ASSIGNMENT`, zero or more `UPDATE` messages, and one `TERMINAL` per slice. Route `ASSIGNMENT` from the dispatcher to the Slice Owner. Route `UPDATE` and `TERMINAL` back only from that owner for that slice. Limit `UPDATE` to freeze or review start, repair start, a blocker or decision, or user-requested status. Require `TERMINAL` to certify that neither the owner nor started work can write to the checkout. Require every message to name its slice identifier. Verify each `TERMINAL` against the active assignment. Require a candidate commit for `COMPLETE`; verify any candidate reported for another status. Retain the active slice identifier, Slice Owner, checkout, base commit, assigned branch, and dependencies until accepting `TERMINAL` or until the platform confirms that the owner cannot continue and the original admission checks pass. Clear the assignment before another `ASSIGNMENT`. Do not persist workflow state or reports.

Retain one end-to-end Slice Owner per slice, a frozen commit, a mandatory independent systemic gate, a resolver, focused auditors, fast validation during editing, and slower validation after stabilization. Require systemic assurance to review raw candidate evidence first, without previous focused-review conclusions, and to inspect every relevant authoritative document and implementation surface needed for a complete system-wide determination. Require systemic assurance to evaluate end-to-end relationships and emergent behavior, and focused reviewers to evaluate lens-owned invariants. Permit both to inspect the same evidence, but prohibit the systemic determination from substituting for focused review of a materially changed lens-owned invariant. Require the systemic reviewer to produce a focused review plan only when `Findings` and `Evidence Gaps` are both `None`, and require the smallest sufficient lens set. Select a lens only when the candidate materially changes a directly owned behavior, contract, or invariant, supplied evidence makes a concrete failure mode reachable, the consequence would materially affect the outcome or an applicable contract, and the lens supplies an independent specialized determination of that distinct risk. When risks overlap, select the lens that most directly owns the root failure and omit lenses that would review only another consequence of the same causal sequence; another planned lens justifies omission only when the omitted lens owns no separate changed material invariant. Prohibit selection based only on an imaginable issue, related files, technologies, keywords, hypothetical future scale, missing evidence, or cross-boundary effects. Permit `Selection: None` only when the candidate materially changes no focused lens-owned invariant. Require missing evidence to produce an evidence gap instead of broader selection. Require systemic findings to be `REQUIRED`; any replacement candidate must restart assurance at the systemic gate.

After a matching systemic pass, require the Slice Owner to accept the plan only when it names distinct canonical reviewers, provides the four-part trigger and distinct contribution for every selection, rejects overlapping consequence-only selections, and provides candidate-specific omission evidence that each unselected lens owns no materially changed invariant or owns only a secondary consequence of a root failure directly owned by a planned lens. Reject systemic coverage as omission evidence. Prohibit the Slice Owner from adding, removing, or substituting reviewers. When the plan selects reviewers, require one focused wave containing every planned auditor for the frozen candidate. Require the owner to start members until no agent execution slot remains, keep unstarted members pending in the same wave, and start one whenever the platform reports an available slot before later assurance work. Lack of slots must not split the wave, change its candidate, permit omission, or constitute failure. Define eventual start and join as conditional on the platform eventually reporting capacity and reviewer completion or inability; prohibit a claim of platform liveness while it supplies none. During an external stall, retain pending members, keep the candidate immutable, and prohibit repair or `COMPLETE`. Give each member only needed raw evidence, withhold the systemic-review conclusion, require an exact candidate match and reviewed revision, and retain early results. Define wave completion as every member returning a matching result. Require the Slice Owner, not the systemic reviewer, to reconcile overlapping focused findings after the complete wave. Do not cancel or delay a pending member because another reports a finding. Require each auditor to return `Findings` and `Evidence Gaps`; a passing result uses `None` for both. If gaps remain after resolution, require `BLOCKED` until a fresh matching result. If new evidence changes plan applicability, restart systemic assurance. If the platform reports that a planned auditor cannot start or return, require completed stop handling and `BLOCKED` unless that auditor supplied a matching result. Skip the wave only when the plan proves that the candidate materially changes no focused lens-owned invariant.

After a systemic finding result or complete focused wave with `REQUIRED` findings, require the Slice Owner to deduplicate equivalent findings and group `REPAIR` findings by root cause and dependency. Do not record a `DEFERRABLE` finding from a candidate that requires repair. Apply all mutually compatible authorized groups in one repair batch; separate groups only for conflict, dependency, or isolated validation needed for a defensible result. Serialize all repair writes. Route `REQUIRED` `DECISION` findings through the resolver when triggered. Validate and commit each repair batch, then restart assurance with systemic review of the replacement candidate. Keep the Slice Owner as the checkout’s only writer.

From candidate freeze until the complete focused wave joins, prohibit every participant, including the System Owner, from modifying the deferral sink. When that wave has no `REQUIRED` finding or evidence gap, require explicit System Owner acceptance under delegated authority before deferring each `DEFERRABLE` finding. Prohibit sink content from granting or proving acceptance. Require the Slice Owner to perform the deferral sink's idempotent append-or-validate operation for each accepted finding, read back the exact record, and retain its identifier. Require `BLOCKED` for write, flush, parse, identity, authority, conflict, or non-owner-access failure; treat unavailable authority as `DECISION_REQUIRED`. Require the Slice Owner to commit all newly appended records in one ledger-only completion commit that has the reviewed candidate as its parent and changes no other path. Treat that commit as review output that preserves the matching assurance results, use it as the final commit for `COMPLETE` and the next slice's base, and invalidate assurance for any other sink modification, path change, or pre-join sink write. A focused result is satisfied when it passes or every finding has one matching accepted deferral record. Require `COMPLETE` to have a matching systemic pass and a satisfied result from every planned focused auditor for the reviewed candidate. When `Selection` is `None`, require only the matching systemic pass.

If a called role cannot return, require the Slice Owner to request a platform stop and return `BLOCKED`. Require the dispatcher to keep the active Slice Owner running until `TERMINAL`, continue the same Slice Owner when the platform can resume it, and not treat elapsed time, absence of an `UPDATE`, or a resumable platform pause as owner loss. When the platform reports that the retained Slice Owner cannot continue the active slice, require the dispatcher to request a platform stop for that owner and slice. Start no other writer until the platform confirms that the retained Slice Owner and all work it started, directly or indirectly, can no longer write to the checkout. Treat any other confirmation as missing, require `BLOCKED`, and preserve the checkout. If the Slice Owner returns `TERMINAL` before confirmation, require the dispatcher to handle that result and ignore later stop confirmation. Otherwise, after confirmation, require the dispatcher to rerun the original admission checks. If they pass, require the dispatcher to clear the assignment and assign the outstanding outcome as a new slice. If they fail, require `BLOCKED` with the failed check and preserve the checkout. Do not create retry scheduling or recovery state.

Verify systemic depth, smallest-sufficient selection with the four-part threshold and distinct contribution, candidate-specific omissions, simplicity, quality, efficiency, message and state limits, capacity-aware concurrent read-only waves with serialized repair writes, and absence of workflow artifacts other than accepted deferral records. Require the dispatcher to request a `DECISION_REQUIRED` decision and report a `BLOCKED` clearing condition or `FAILED` criterion with evidence. After `DECISION_REQUIRED` or `FAILED`, prohibit another `ASSIGNMENT` until the user authorizes the next action. After `BLOCKED`, prohibit another `ASSIGNMENT` until objective evidence proves every reported condition cleared or the user authorizes an alternative that avoids those conditions.
